Third-party components
=====================

Model: cohere-transcribe-arabic-07-2026 (Q8_0 GGUF)
  Original model by Cohere Labs: https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-arabic-07-2026
  GGUF conversion: https://huggingface.co/handy-computer/cohere-transcribe-arabic-07-2026-gguf
  Licence: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  Not included in this repository. Bring your own copy, or setup.cmd downloads it
  from the Hugging Face page above.

runtime\transcribe.dll, ggml*.dll
  transcribe.cpp (https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp) and ggml
  (https://github.com/ggml-org/ggml), as shipped with the Handy app
  (https://github.com/cjpais/Handy, MIT licence). MIT licence.

runtime\msvcp140*.dll, vcruntime140*.dll, vcomp140.dll
  Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable runtime files, redistributable with applications.

Python packages installed by setup.cmd (numpy, scipy, soundfile, fastapi, uvicorn,
python-multipart, imageio-ffmpeg) come from PyPI under their own open-source licences.
